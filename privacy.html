This policy explains what information the mobile game Impossible Kick (Android package com.GoToTheMoon.ImpossibleFreekick, and the iOS version) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by GoToTheMoon ("we").

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or messages. We do not sell personal data.

2. Third-party services

The game uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Analytics) – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads – unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Game Center and App Store – apple.com/legal/privacy

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads. Ad partners may use your device's advertising ID to show more relevant ads. You can reset this ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). A one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") turns off forced ads.

4. Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We keep your game data for as long as you play. If an account has been inactive for 24 months, or when you ask us to delete it, we delete it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: see Delete my data.
  • Analytics: you can turn off analytics data collection in the game's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, Türkiye under KVKK, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Write to us to use these rights.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will also be announced in the game.

8. Contact
